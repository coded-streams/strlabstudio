# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making Str:::lab Studio a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful project for everyone.

We value participation from contributors of all backgrounds regardless of experience level, identity, or geography.

---

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussions
- Focus on technical merit
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Prioritize collaboration over conflict
- Help new contributors learn and grow

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discriminatory language
- Personal attacks or insults
- Trolling or disruptive behavior
- Sharing private information without consent
- Dominating discussions in bad faith

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may:
- Issue warnings
- Temporarily restrict contributions
- Remove access for repeated violations

All enforcement actions must be documented publicly when applicable.

---

## Reporting Issues

Report violations by opening a private security issue or contacting maintainers directly.
